## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-energy, nocturnal street-grill atmosphere translated into a polished culinary digital experience. Tailored for an audience of fast-moving food lovers, late-night diners, families, and youth culture across Kerala seeking authentic charcoal Mandhi, smoky Alfam, and wok-tossed street specialties, the identity centers on heat, smokiness, hunger arousal, and swift utility.

### Visual Aesthetic
The aesthetic balances **Dark Mode Culinary Immersion** with **Modern Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- **Ember & Charcoal Foundations:** Deep, matte volcanic slates substitute standard black, evoking pit ovens, tandoors, and grill grates without straining eyes under low-light evening ordering.
- **Sizzle & Flame Accents:** High-chroma fiery gradients mimic live embers and searing flames to trigger appetite and drive focal priority directly toward action buttons and key dishes.
- **Fresh Herb Contrast:** Crisp garden mint/emerald highlights balance heavy smoked meat tones, signaling freshness in salads, platters, veg indicators, and active preparation statuses.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Frosted glass headers, layered dark containers, and localized ember glows yield depth without visual clutter, keeping the focus squarely on imagery and fast ordering.

## Colors

The palette establishes an appetizing nocturnal grill aesthetic. The hierarchy is strictly engineered around culinary contrast: deep charcoal baselines, searing ember accents, and vivid garden garnishes.

### Core Roles & Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FF5722` - Ember Red-Orange):** The hero action color. Used for primary CTAs (Quick Add, Checkout), interactive badges, and hot tags.
- **Secondary (`#FF7A00` - Flare Orange):** Radiance and highlight color. Drives hover transitions, flaming gradient terminals, badge borders, and spice meter indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Mint Herb Green):** Contrast, vitality, and utility. Explicitly reserved for pure vegetarian designations, successful cart additions, live order dispatch tracking, and halal guarantee seals.
- **Neutral Base (`#0F1115` - Deep Smoked Slate):** Screen backdrop. Richer and warmer than standard `#000000`, absorbing harsh glare while emphasizing food photography.

### Tonal Surfaces
- **Surface Canvas:** `#0F1115` (Deepest canvas)
- **Surface Raised / Card Background:** `#161920` (Food cards, bottom sheets, navigation bars)
- **Surface Hover / Highlight:** `#222631` (Elevated states, selected tab backdrops)
- **Border Subtle:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Border Ember Accent:** `rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.35)`

### Semantic Gradients
- **Flame Sizzle Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF5722 0%, #FF7A00 100%)` for hero promotions, active order triggers, and signature Mandhi/Alfam badges.
- **Smoky Glass Surface:** `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(22, 25, 32, 0.85) 0%, rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.95) 100%)` with backdrop filter.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Outfit** (display & pricing) with **Inter** (menu descriptions, meta details, ingredients, and operational labels).

### Principles
- **Punchy Display:** `Outfit` carries high geometric energy with tight kerning for section headlines, hero promos, and prominent Malayalam/Arabic-inspired fusion menu categories.
- **Clean Utility:** `Inter` handles dense item ingredients, portion options (Half/Full), customization selectors, and allergen callouts without visual fatigue.
- **Price Legibility:** Menu item pricing is consistently set in `Outfit Bold` (`title-price`) to make monetary calculations effortless under quick scanning.
- **Numeric Rhythm:** Enable tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across all price tags and cart counters to avoid layout jank during item increments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 8pt geometric grid with fluid flex adaptions for mobile-first dining interactions.

### Breakpoints & Layout Architecture
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin: `16px`, Gutter: `12px`. Food cards default to single-column horizontal list cards or a 2-column compact grid. The bottom of the viewport accommodates a permanent 72px safe clearing for the floating cart pill tray.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Margin: `24px`, Gutter: `16px`. Menu cards render in a balanced 2-to-3 column grid with sticky horizontal category tabs below the nav.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout. Max container width: `1280px`. Margin: `32px`, Gutter: `24px`. Converts the floating mobile cart tray into a sticky split-screen order summary panel alongside a 3-column food item showcase.

### Vertical Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-2xs` to `space-xs`):** Used between spice badges, veg/non-veg dots, and item sub-labels.
- **Component Padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`):** Food card interior padding and category pill tap targets.
- **Section Spacing (`space-xl` to `space-3xl`):** Distinction between distinct culinary zones (e.g., Charcoal Alfam Showcase vs Chinese Wok Delights).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient flame luminescence**, dark stacked tonal surfaces, and **frosted obsidian glass**. White drop shadows are completely absent; instead, light sources emit either faint ember warmth or pure atmospheric occlusion.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level 0 (Ground Canvas):** `#0F1115` with subtle background radial grain or faint 5% ember ambient glow at the top edges.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#161920` surface with a 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Focus):** `#1C202A` surface with an illuminated border `rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.35)` and a localized flame glow: `0 8px 30px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.16)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays & Cart Tray):** Obsidian glassmorphic surface with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, background `rgba(22, 25, 32, 0.82)`, and boundary glow: `0 12px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Sheets & Customizer Drawers):** `#1A1D26` elevated bottom-sheet with top accent rim: `0 -1px 0 rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.4)` and ambient room darkening scrim `rgba(10, 11, 15, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Pill-Shaped (`roundedness: 3`)** foundational geometry. Generous curves soften the deep dark-mode palette, making the interface touch-friendly, energetic, and tactile.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Base Pill Radius (`9999px` / Full Pill):** Primary action buttons, quick-add incrementors, filter chips, category tabs, and spice meter capsules.
- **Food Card Containers (`24px` / `rounded-xl`):** Curving generously to frame food photography and contain portion customization grids cleanly.
- **Badges & Meta Counters (`12px`):** Used on non-veg/veg indicators, portion tags (Quarter / Half / Full), and discount labels.
- **Interactive Micro-controls (`8px`):** Checkbox options inside addon modals (extra Kuboos, Garlic Mayo, Alfam Masala Dip).

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Sizzle CTA):** Full pill shape, background gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF5722 0%, #FF7A00 100%)`, text `#FFFFFF` (`Outfit Bold`), shadow `0 4px 18px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.35)`. Active scale feedback of `0.97`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Outline:** Dark surface background `#161920`, border `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `#F3F4F6`. Hover triggers border transition to `#FF5722` with text color shift.
- **Quick-Add Button:** Compact pill button. Default: `+ Add` with subtle flame border. On click: morphs smoothly into an expanded quantity stepper (`- [count] +`) with `#222631` fill and `#FF5722` symbols.

### 2. Category Navigation Chips
- Horizontal scrolling dock with concealed scrollbars and snap alignment.
- **Default State:** Pill shape, background `#161920`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#9CA3AF` (`label-md`).
- **Active State:** Background `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.18), rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.12))`, border `1.5px solid #FF5722`, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by a subtle orange underglow.

### 3. Food Item Cards
- Surface background `#161920` with `24px` corner radiuses.
- **Hero Imagery:** Upper 55% height, showcasing crisp aspect ratio photos with a bottom dark gradient overlay (`rgba(22, 25, 32, 0) to #161920`) ensuring effortless legibility of overlay tags.
- **Dietary Marker:** Kerala/FSSAI compliant square icon (Green dot inside green border for Veg, Red triangle inside brown border for Non-Veg).
- **Spice Flame Meter:** 1 to 3 pepper/flame glyphs colored in `#FF7A00` to indicate heat level (e.g., Mild Shawarma vs Fiery Peri-Peri Alfam).
- **Pricing & Action Footer:** Bottom row aligning bold price (`Outfit`) next to the pill `Add` stepper button.

### 4. Floating Order / Cart Tray
- Sticky bottom dock floating `16px` above the bottom screen boundary, centered with a max-width of `560px`.
- Backdrop: Glassmorphic obsidian slate `rgba(22, 25, 32, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and perimeter border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Left: Item count counter badge (`#FF5722`) and subtotal price.
- Right: "View Cart / Checkout" pill button with forward chevron and pulse-glow indicator on new item entry.

### 5. Input Fields & Search
- Rounded `9999px` search pill with magnifying glass glyph in `#9CA3AF`.
- Background `#161920`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Focus state illuminates border to `#FF5722` with an ambient ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.18)`. Placeholder text in `#6B7280`.

### 6. Portion / Add-on Selectors (Mandhi / Alfam Customizers)
- Segmented pill controls for meat portions: `Quarter`, `Half`, `Full`.
- Unselected: Surface `#161920`, text `#9CA3AF`.
- Selected: Surface `#FF5722`, text `#FFFFFF`, weight `Outfit 600`.
- Checkbox list for accompaniments (Extra Kuboos, Mayo dip, Mint Chutney) using a rounded box (`8px`) with checkfill in `#10B981`.
